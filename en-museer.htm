Simply Scandinavia produces pocelain for several Swedish museum shops such as the Royal Theater at Drottningholm and Solliden Castle. The majority of the designs are inspired by objects in the collections of the respective museums.

Many shapes to choose from

Simply Scandinavia can help you with anything from soap dishes to large urns and the design can be had in many colours or just one.
